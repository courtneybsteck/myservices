Courtney Steck, LPC

Licensed Professional Counselor

Life can be complicated, and finding your way forward isn't always easy. You might be struggling emotionally, moving through a difficult transition, or feeling stuck in patterns that no longer serve you. Perhaps you’re noticing how past experiences or trauma continue to shape the way you connect with others. You're ready for something to change, even if the next step feels uncertain. This is where our work together begins.

I'm committed to creating a warm, collaborative space where you can show up as your complete, authentic self. My goal is to truly understand you and your story in all its complexity—not just the challenges you're facing, but also your strengths, values, and vision for the life you want. I’m here to listen deeply and offer thoughtful perspective, so you can gain insights from your past and envision new possibilities for the future.

Therapy shouldn’t be one-size-fits-all, so I draw from different approaches based on your unique needs and preferences. Together, we’ll map out a path that supports your goals while remaining flexible enough to evolve as you do. And even when we’re navigating difficult terrain, there will be room for humor and moments of joy along the way. [Supervised by Daron Teske, LCPC]

Therapeutic Approaches: Psychodynamic and attachment-based therapies, cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), acceptance and commitment therapy (ACT), dialectical behavior therapy (DBT), mindfulness and somatic practices

If you'd like to get a sense of whether we might be a good fit, you're welcome to start with a free 15-minute consultation call. Or if you're ready to begin, you can schedule your first session directly. I look forward to connecting with you!
