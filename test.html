Life can be complicated, and finding your way forward isn't always easy. You might be struggling emotionally, moving through a difficult transition, or feeling stuck in patterns that no longer serve you. Perhaps you’re noticing how past experiences or trauma continue to shape the way you connect with others. You're ready for something to change, even if the next step feels uncertain. This is where our work together begins.

I'm committed to creating a warm, collaborative space where you can show up as your complete, authentic self. My goal is to truly understand you and your story in all its complexity. I’m here to listen deeply and offer thoughtful perspective, so you can gain insights from your past and envision new possibilities for the future.

Therapy shouldn't be one-size-fits-all, so I draw from different approaches based on your needs and preferences. Together, we'll map out a path that supports your goals while staying flexible enough to evolve as you do. And even when we're navigating difficult terrain, there will be room for humor along the way. I look forward to connecting with you!

Ready to Connect?

I'm accepting new clients for both in-person and telehealth sessions at Leap Therapy Chicago, located at 1770 West Berteau Avenue in the Ravenswood neighborhood of Chicago.

I offer a free 15-minute consultation as a chance for you to share a bit about what you're looking for in therapy and get a sense of my approach. From there, you can decide if working together would feel like a good fit.
